MAYTERA ARENA - ART ASSET CREDITS
=================================================================
This file covers two different asset sets under two different
licenses; read each section's License line, not just this intro.

The world/environment graphics overhaul (textures, sky, signage
below) uses freely-licensed art from OpenGameArt.org. Every asset
in THAT section is CC0 (public domain). CC0 requires no
attribution; credit is given here voluntarily and because this
repository is public.

The character and weapon sprites (see "Freedoom Character and
Weapon Sprites" below) are a SEPARATE set under the Freedoom
project's BSD 3-clause license, which DOES require attribution -
this section exists to satisfy that.
